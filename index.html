✦ The Timeline

Growing & Building

I joined the Examination Office in January 2022, expecting to manage files and coordinate routine tasks. I remained for four and a half years, during which I had the privilege of building systems that meaningfully changed how our office worked.

The OCE was a demanding environment in which precision mattered greatly. Over time, I noticed gaps in our processes and began developing tools to address them, not only to ease my own responsibilities, but to support the entire team.

From internal meetings to the coordination of complex operations, this journey was defined by continuous learning. Each challenge became an opportunity to refine our workflows and contribute to the team's success.
